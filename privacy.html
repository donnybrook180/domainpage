Privacy Policy for Sonatris Limited apps

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Sonatris Limited ("we", "our", or "us") operates several mobile applications (the "Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

1. Information Collection and Use

We take your privacy seriously. We do not run servers that receive your data, and we do not collect, store, or sell any personal information. You can use our apps without an account and without giving us your name, email address, or contact details.

What you enter in an app, such as habits, workouts, cycle or pregnancy details, baby logs, medicines, symptoms, weights, notes and photos, is stored on your device. Some of this can be health information. It only leaves your device in the cases described below: when you turn on Google Drive backup, when you share with someone you choose, or when you export it yourself (for example a PDF summary for your midwife or doctor).

2. Advertising

Our free apps show advertisements from AdMob (provided by Google). AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, your approximate location (derived from your IP address), and information about how you use the app and its ads, to show ads, measure them, and prevent fraud. Your health information and the content you enter are never shared with AdMob.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other regions where it is required, the app asks for your consent before ads are personalised, and you can change your choice later in the app's settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings. Premium removes the ads. For more information on how Google uses data for advertising, see Google's Privacy & Terms.

3. Purchases

In-app purchases and subscriptions are handled by Google Play (or the Apple App Store). We do not receive your payment details. The app only learns from the store whether you own Premium, so it can unlock it.

4. Google Drive Backup and Sync

Some of our apps offer an optional feature to back up your data and keep it in sync between your devices, using your own Google Drive account. This feature is only used if you choose to sign in with Google and turn it on.

When you use this feature, your data is transferred directly between your device and your Google Drive account, into a hidden app folder that only that app can read. We do not receive, store, or have access to your backup data or your Google account. You can revoke the app's access to your Google Drive at any time in your Google account settings. Your use of Google Drive is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Sharing with People You Choose

Some of our apps let you share data with people you invite, for example a pregnancy with your partner, a baby's log with family or a nanny, or a medicine list with a carer. Sharing only happens if you start it:

  • the shared data is kept in a file in the Google Drive of the person who shared it; the app can only see files it created or that you picked;
  • to invite someone you enter their email address, which the app gives to Google Drive to grant them access; Google may email them a notice. We do not receive or keep it;
  • the person you invite opens the invite through our join page at sonatris.org/join, which signs in with Google and opens the shared file in their browser; nothing passes through our servers;
  • only what the app puts in the shared file is shared; for example, My Pregnancy never shares your bump photos;
  • you can remove someone or stop sharing at any time, and anyone who joined can leave at any time.

6. Log Data

We do not monitor or log your activity within the application. No technical data, such as IP addresses or unique device identifiers, is collected or stored by us. Reminders and notifications are scheduled on your device.

7. Keeping and Deleting Your Data

Your data stays until you delete it. Because we do not hold any of it, you can delete all of it yourself:

  • In the app: Profile → Clear all data deletes everything you tracked on this device and, with sync on, on your other synced devices.
  • Google Drive backup: open Google Drive on the web → Settings → Manage apps → the app → Options → Delete hidden app data.
  • Shared files: stop sharing in the app, or delete the shared file or folder from your Google Drive.
  • Google access: remove the app in your Google account settings.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes the data on that device.

Data collected by AdMob is kept and deleted by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. If you need help, email us at the address below.

8. Health Information

Our apps are for information and tracking only. They are not medical devices and do not give medical advice. We never sell your data or use your health information for advertising.

9. Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13, and our pregnancy app is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
